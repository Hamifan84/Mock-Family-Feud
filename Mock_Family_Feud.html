How to Play

TEAMS

Divide into 2 teams of 6-7 people.
Example: "The Spicy Deluxes" vs "The Waffle Fries".

FACE-OFF

One player from each team answers during the face-off. Reveal the answer, choose the team that got it right, and that team takes control. After 3 strikes, the other team gets one steal attempt. A missed steal reveals the remaining answers with no points or extra strike.

FAST MONEY

Fast Money is played by two people. The second player must face away from the screen and must not see the first player's revealed answers. The host records the first player's answers, then the second player answers using only the remaining choices.
